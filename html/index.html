How To Play Speudo

1. Type the word shown before time runs out. Each round gives you a limited window to type it correctly.

2. Correct words build up your multiplier. Every word you type correctly in a row increases your score multiplier, up to a maximum of 3x.

3. Keep the streak alive. A mistake resets your multiplier back to the start. So accuracy matters just as much as speed once your multiplier climbs.

4. Maximize your score. The longer your streak, the more each correct word is worth. Big scores come from staying accurate under pressure, not just typing fast.

5. How scoring works. Each word is worth 1 point per letter, multiplied by your difficulty (Easy x1, Medium x2, Hard x3) and your current streak multiplier (x2 at a 3-word streak, x3 at a 6-word streak). Longer words on higher difficulties with a strong streak are worth the most.
